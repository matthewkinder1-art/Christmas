The area

  • £1,260 leaves nearly £2,750 of the budget unspent
  • Aysgarth Falls has a proper accessible route — compacted path, gentle gradients, a wheelchair viewing platform and all-terrain scooters to hire
  • Leyburn 4½ miles for shops; Middleham's racehorses on the gallops are free to watch from the car
  • Wensleydale Railway runs Mince Pie Specials between Christmas and New Year from Leyburn
  • No en suite in either cottage
  • Nearest pub is half a mile up an unlit single-track hill — not walkable for Nan
  • Bolton Castle and the Forbidden Corner are both shut for our week
Two things to weigh: Field Cottage's bedrooms are up a short stairway, so the aunt and uncle get stairs (fine). And the road up Coverdale is one of the first in the Dales to close in snow — the safe route in and out is east via Middleham, not over the top to Kettlewell whatever the satnav says.
