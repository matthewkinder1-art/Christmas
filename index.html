Six of us, two dogs, and Nan needs a bedroom on the ground floor. Here are the eleven places that actually work — including four in Cornwall — plus 18 Airbnbs confirmed free for our exact week. One page each: have a look and tell me which you like.

On Cornwall

Good news — Cornwall works better than I expected. The pattern that solves our problem is small farm cottage clusters: four or five converted barns round one yard, booked individually. Because they're barn conversions they tend to be single storey, which is exactly what Nan needs, and farm owners are usually relaxed about two dogs.

The south coast strip — Looe, Fowey, the Roseland — turned up plenty of two-dog cottages but almost nothing with a ground-floor bedroom in a two-building site. Everything below is north coast, mid-Cornwall or inland.

Three more Cornish sites worth a call

  • Cory Farm, Morwenstow (nr Bude) — Marsland Cottage sleeps 6 with all three bedrooms downstairs and an en-suite master, 2 dogs. All four of us on one level, aunt and uncle in Welcombe or Stanbury. Remote though — Bude is 25 minutes. Listing ↗
  • Treligga Farm, nr Port Isaac — the cheapest of the lot at roughly £1,400–£2,200 with published rates. Swallows is all ground floor with a wet room and a hot tub; Stable next door sleeps 2. Catch: no en suite, and the second bedroom is bunks. Listing ↗
  • Talehay, Pelynt (nr Looe) — cheapest of all at about £1,625, and the closest to Polperro and Looe. But the two listing sites disagree about which floor Owl Cottage's bedrooms are on, so it needs confirming before anything else. Website ↗

Ruled out in Cornwall: Boscrowan Farm and Downs Barn Farm (no pets at all), The Barn at Constantine and Courtyard Farm at Boscastle (1 dog only), Bosinver near St Austell (dogs only in selected cottages at certain times of year), and Pen Den Har at Kingsand — great layout, but it sleeps 12 and starts at £2,621 a week before the Christmas premium.

🐕 Walking the dogs straight from the door

This turned out to be the most useful question yet — and it splits the list clean in half. Some of these you walk out of the gate onto a footpath. Others, it's the car every single time, twice a day, in the dark, for a week.

Two things I'd not thought about: it gets dark by about 4pm in late December, and most of these are on unlit single-track lanes with no verge. And a lot of "lovely countryside walks" are stile-heavy field paths that are miserable with two dogs and knee-deep in mud by Christmas.

The two that do it all at once

Hoe Grange is the standout. You reach the High Peak Trail across the farm's own fields, so you never touch a road — and the trail is a traffic-free former railway the owners explicitly say you can let the dogs off on. Same surface works for Nan, and they hire out off-road wheelchairs. It's the only site that wins on dogs, road safety, December mud and mobility simultaneously.

Trenarlett does the same trick in Cornwall. The Camel Trail — flat, surfaced, officially wheelchair-rated — starts about 500m from the gate down a quiet dead-end lane. One walk that works for the dogs and Nan together, every day, whatever the weather.

And the two I'd now drop: Long View at Much Marcle, because the A449 runs through the village and there's no verified walk from the door. And West Close at Melmerby — steep unlit single-track, no pavement, no flat option for Nan, and a real chance of being snowed in.

Two rules worth knowing

  • Cornish beach dog bans are seasonal and lifted by December. Perranporth, Widemouth, Summerleaze, Crooklets, Sandymouth and Crinnis are all off-lead in Christmas week. Daymer Bay has no restrictions at any time of year.
  • Trenarlett and Hele Barton both ban leaving dogs alone in the cottage. That's worth thinking about before booking a Christmas lunch out somewhere.

Who to ring, in order

Reordered 23 August 2026 after re-checking every Devon and Cornwall listing at source. Two things moved: Harrods went up, Trenarlett came down.

  1. Harrods Cornish Cottages Penhallow, near Truro moved up
    Now the best layout match in Cornwall. Coach House + The Forge gives two buildings with every bedroom on the ground floor and en suite, for roughly £1,380–£2,950. Ask: is 21–28 Dec free for both, what is the festive rate, and — the one conflict we could not settle — does The Forge have a stepped entrance or is it genuinely all on one level?
  2. Hoe Grange Brassington, Peak District
    Still the best accessibility anywhere and the best dog walking — the traffic-free trail is reached across the farm's own fields. Ask: the Christmas price, which they have never published, and whether two cabins are free together.
  3. Trenarlett Farm St Tudy, Cornwall moved down
    Lead with the steps. The listing says three steps sit between the lounge and the second bedroom, avoidable only by going outside. Ask that first: if the step-free bedroom is not the one Nan would use, this drops off the list and the rest of the call is wasted.
  4. Low Costa Mill Pickering — 01751 472050
    The only confirmed Christmas price on the list at £1,700, any changeover day. Ask: which third cottage is free that week, because Farrier's + Carter's leaves Matthew without a bed.
  5. Tregrehan Garden Cottages Par, Cornwall
    Ask one thing: the Game Keepers rate for 21–28 Dec. The £1,060 they publish is the New Year week, which starts the day ours ends. Coach House is settled at £1,540.
  6. Wooder Manor Widecombe — 01364 621391
    The only place Nan could walk to a pub. Ask: the Christmas rate, and make sure she gets Lower Hameldown's wheel-in shower bedroom — the second double's en suite is up two steps.
  7. Hele Barton Farm Week St Mary — 01288 341847
    Only worth ringing if the upside-down layout is acceptable — Mowhay's living room is upstairs, so Nan climbs stairs to sit with everyone. Ask: whether Cyder Barn is the right way up, and about winter heating, after a December review reported it cold and damp.

Not a phone call: the Airbnb options are bookable on the spot — Danby Cottage (£1,640), Fernleigh (£1,647) and Barn Cottage (£1,075) are confirmed free for our week right now. They are the fallback if the calls go badly, but they will go to someone else if we sit on them.

Before we book

Two things nobody has confirmed yet

Availability. None of these sites will show a live Christmas 2026 calendar, so I can't tell you whether any of them are actually free. Christmas cottages that take two dogs and have a ground-floor bedroom are a small pool — they go early. Ringing beats emailing.

Christmas pricing. Only Low Costa Mill (£1,700) and West Close (£1,260) publish a festive rate. Everything else is a "from" price, and Christmas weeks typically run 1.5–2.5× that. Even so, six of the seven should land well under £4,000.

Questions for whoever rings

  1. Can we have two dogs in the main cottage? Get it written into the booking — this is what ruled out half the shortlist.
  2. Is Nan's bedroom step-free from the car, not just step-free once you're inside?
  3. How many yards between the two buildings, and is it lit and level at night?
  4. Is the garden properly enclosed for the dogs?
  5. Log fire or wood-burner — and is the fuel included over Christmas?
  6. Can both cottages be held as one booking so we don't lose one and keep the other?
  7. What's the changeover day? Low Costa Mill is any day; others are fixed Saturday or Tuesday, which changes our dates.
  8. Low Costa Mill only: what third cottage is free that week for Matthew?
