Airbnb — Cornwall, Devon & the Cotswolds

Searched 23 August 2026 for 21–28 December 2026, 6 guests, 2 dogs, 3+ bedrooms. Everything below was confirmed free for our exact week at the price shown — the one thing no cottage agency would tell us.

Cornwall

about 20 places matched the filters. None with a separate building. Checked all 18 read: none has an annexe or second building.

  • Brayside — converted chapel, St Just Ground-floor bedroom £1,700
    Two bedrooms on the ground floor (super king + zip-and-link). Master en suite is upstairs. The best ground-floor layout in Cornwall — Nan and Mum & Dad could both be downstairs.
  • Beachfront home overlooking Porth Beach Ground-floor bedroom £1,684
    Third bedroom is ground floor, double, with a shower room on the same level. Other two bedrooms upstairs.
  • Beachside family home, Praa Sands Ground-floor bedroom £3,075
    Bedroom One is ground floor, super king, converts to twins. Dog friendly in the listing title.

Devon

18 places matched the filters. 1 with a separate building. The only genuine separate building found anywhere in this search.

  • Fernleigh — Georgeham, near Croyde Separate building £1,647
    The only true separate building found in all three regions. A 2-bedroom cottage plus a third bedroom in a separate annexe — a self-contained double with en-suite WC/shower. Two great pubs and a village store walkable. The catch: three bedrooms total, so with the aunt and uncle in the annexe, Nan, Mum & Dad and Matthew share just two rooms — one pair too few. And the annexe is a bedroom, not a living space.
  • Barn Cottage — Westover Farm, Wootton Fitzpaine Ground-floor bedroom £1,075
    Ground-floor twin bedroom with its own en-suite shower room — exactly Nan's requirement, and the cheapest thing in this whole search. Private fenced secure garden, parking for three. The site has a second cottage, Cider Cottage, which the listing says can be booked at the same time — but Cider is not available our week. Worth ringing to ask if that changes. Dorset, 3 miles from Lyme Regis.
  • 5 The Vista — hot tub, pool Ground-floor bedroom £2,347
    Two ground-floor bedrooms: Bedroom One king with en-suite shower and direct access to a decked area, Bedroom Two ground-floor zip-and-link twin. Best ground-floor layout in Devon.
  • Fantastic Beach House, Hope Cove Ground-floor bedroom £2,432
    Bedrooms on the ground floor per the listing. Exceptional location.
  • the Smithy + the Stables — near Charmouth Two cottages, one site £664 + £761
    Two of three cottages on one site (refs UKC3748/3749/3750), shared lawned garden, both free our week — £1,425 for the pair, the cheapest two-building option anywhere here. The catch: both put every bedroom on the first floor, so there is nothing on the ground floor for Nan.

Cotswolds

18 places matched the filters. None with a separate building. No second buildings, but the single best accessible property in the whole search.

  • Danby Cottage — Forest of Dean, Lydney Best for Nan £1,640
    The strongest find of this refresh. Extended in 2007 with accessibility designed in, not retrofitted: fully wheelchair accessible, downstairs bedroom and bathroom, garden with level access, lowered worktop, wide doorways. Gold Green Tourism Award. Forest walks straight from the door for the dogs, and forest walks, cycle paths, parks and pubs all within walking distance — which is more than Hoe Grange manages. Confirmed £1,640 for our exact week with two dogs. The catch: three bedrooms, one building — no separate space for the aunt and uncle.
  • The Coach House — Coopers Hill, Brockworth Ground-floor bedroom £1,738
    Renovated 18th-century coach house sleeping six across three bedrooms, ground-floor bedroom, log burner. Peaceful spot on Coopers Hill.
  • Swallow Cottage — Olivemead Farm, Chippenham Ground-floor bedroom £2,242
    Bedroom 1 is ground floor, king, right next to a ground-floor bathroom. Hot tub, large garden. Two cautions: dogs are “welcome on request” rather than guaranteed, and the owners ask that dogs stay on a lead outside the cottage because of wildlife — which undoes the point of a garden for two dogs.

Everything available in Cornwall

Across all three regions, exactly one property has a separate building — Fernleigh at Georgeham — and it is one bedroom short. Every other listing read is a single house: nowhere for the aunt and uncle to sleep apart from everyone else. On Airbnb the route to two buildings is booking two listings on one site, which is what the eleven cottages already do; the Smithy + Stables pair below is the one case of that we found free for our week.

Ground-floor bedrooms fared better — 3 of the 18 in Cornwall and several in Devon and the Cotswolds, all called out above.
