Two places, a house or two apart

Since a 2-bed for Nan and the dogs plus a 2-bed nearby is acceptable, this is the short answer. Everything here is confirmed free for 21–28 December 2026 and takes two dogs, at the prices shown on 23 August 2026.

Best fit

Otters Holt + Badgers Sett

Towan, near St Merryn & Constantine Bay · £2,423 for the week

  • Otters Holt (£1,005) — Nan, Mum & Dad and both dogs. A bungalow, so everything is on one level. The primary bedroom is a king with its own en suite and sliding doors straight onto the garden — Nan gets a ground-floor room, her own bathroom, and the dogs go out without going through the house. Large enclosed rear garden with decking and lawn. Second bedroom takes a king plus bunks.
  • Badgers Sett (£1,418) — Matthew and the aunt and uncle. Detached, private south-facing garden, parking for three, two doubles upstairs (one king, one double-plus-single). Ground floor has a cloakroom and a boot-room entrance hall.
  • Both are in the same small hamlet, Towan — a house or two apart, exactly the arrangement you said would be fine. Both take dogs.
  • Walkable village: St Merryn is an easy walk from both, with a mini market, bakery and restaurants. Footpaths run from Otters Holt's door through fields towards Trevose Head, so the dogs get a walk without a car. Constantine Bay beach is close; Padstow is a three-mile drive.
  • £2,423 the pair — £1,577 under budget, and the cheapest arrangement found that actually satisfies the brief.

Runner-up

Choca + Kegyn, Polmark Beach Cottages

Harlyn Bay, 200m from the beach · £2,868 for the week

  • Two cottages in one small development with communal courtyard gardens, a few hundred metres from Harlyn Bay — the tightest "one or two houses away" of anything found.
  • The problem is Nan. Both put every bedroom on the first floor: Choca's are up "corner stairs", Kegyn's are upstairs with pets restricted to the ground floor. There is no ground-floor bedroom in either.
  • Worth a call only if a ground-floor bed could be arranged, or if another Polmark unit is free.

Runner-up

Streatfeild + Nantucket at Highcliffe

Trebetherick, near Polzeath · £4,281 for the week

  • The closest thing to a proper estate. Five houses on one site with a shared gym, tennis courts, yoga studio and a staffed reception; the coast path runs to Polzeath and Daymer Bay. Four houses free our week.
  • Nantucket has two bedrooms downstairs — the only Highcliffe listing that says so. Streatfeild's and Fastnet's do not state which floor, so that needs asking.
  • £4,281 puts it £281 over budget, and dogs are £25 each per week on top. The nicest setting of the three, and the only one that fails on money.

One house, not a pair

42 Dennis Road

Padstow · £1,840 for the week

  • Not a pair, but worth keeping in view: all three bedrooms are on the ground floor, one en suite, plus a second ground-floor shower room — and the kitchen, dining and sitting rooms are downstairs too, so nobody climbs stairs to be together. The upstairs is only a lounge with an estuary view.
  • It houses Nan, Mum & Dad and Matthew comfortably. The aunt and uncle would need something nearby — and nothing adjacent to it came up free.
