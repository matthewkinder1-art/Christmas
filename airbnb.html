Two places, a house or two apart

Since a 2-bed for Nan and the dogs plus a 2-bed nearby is acceptable, this is the short answer. Everything here is confirmed free for 21–28 December 2026 and takes two dogs, at the prices shown on 23 August 2026.

The rule everything is filtered on: no upstairs bedroom for Nan. Nan does not sleep upstairs. Anything without a genuine ground-floor bedroom is out, whatever else it has going for it — every option below was checked by reading the listing's own room-by-room description, not by trusting the summary.

Best fit

Otters Holt + Badgers Sett

Towan, near St Merryn & Constantine Bay · £2,423 for the week

  • Otters Holt (£1,005) — Nan, Mum & Dad and both dogs. It is a bungalow, so there is no upstairs at all — the question cannot arise. The primary bedroom is a king with its own en suite and sliding doors straight onto the garden: ground floor, own bathroom, and the dogs go out without coming through the house. Large enclosed rear garden with decking and lawn. Second bedroom takes a king plus bunks.
  • Badgers Sett (£1,418) — Matthew and the aunt and uncle. Detached, private south-facing garden, parking for three. Its two doubles are upstairs — which is fine, because Nan is not in this one.
  • Same small hamlet, Towan — a house or two apart, and both take two dogs.
  • Walkable village: St Merryn is an easy walk from both, with a mini market, bakery and restaurants. Footpaths run from Otters Holt's door through fields towards Trevose Head, so the dogs get walked without a car. Constantine Bay is close; Padstow is three miles.
  • £2,423 the pair — £1,577 under budget, and the only arrangement found that satisfies the ground-floor rule at a sensible price.

Runner-up

Streatfeild + Nantucket at Highcliffe

Trebetherick, near Polzeath · £4,281 for the week

  • Only works one way round: Nan must be in Nantucket, which has two bedrooms downstairs. Streatfeild's listing never says which floor its bedrooms are on, so it cannot be assumed — ask before booking.
  • The nicest setting of the lot: five houses on one estate with a shared gym, tennis courts and a staffed reception, and the coast path running to Polzeath and Daymer Bay.
  • £4,281 is £281 over budget, plus £25 per dog per week. The only shortlisted option that fails on money.

One house, not a pair

42 Dennis Road

Padstow · £1,840 for the week

  • One house rather than a pair, but it clears the rule outright: all three bedrooms are on the ground floor, one en suite, plus a second ground-floor shower room. The kitchen, dining and sitting rooms are downstairs too, so nobody climbs stairs to be together — the upstairs is only a lounge with an estuary view.
  • Houses Nan, Mum & Dad and Matthew. The aunt and uncle would need something nearby, and nothing adjacent came up free for our week.

Fails the rule

Ruled out by the rule — Choca + Kegyn at Polmark

Harlyn Bay · was £2,868 for the week

  • Kept here so nobody re-finds it and gets excited. Physically it is the best arrangement of all — two cottages sharing a courtyard garden, 200m from the beach.
  • But every bedroom in both is upstairs. Choca's are up corner stairs; Kegyn's are upstairs with pets confined to the ground floor. There is no ground-floor bed for Nan in either, so it is out.
  • Same fate for two Padstow cottages that looked promising on price — Hillcrest (£998) and Sea Meadows (£1,075). Both read well until the room list: both bedrooms upstairs in each.
