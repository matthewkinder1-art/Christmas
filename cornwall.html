Before anyone books

Three things to check

  1. Step-free from the car, not just inside. Every listing above has a ground-floor bed; none of them promises a level path from the parking space. One message to the host settles it.
  2. Two dogs in writing. The pet filter was on for every search, but get it stated on the booking — it's the rule that eliminated half of Cornwall.
  3. Prices move. Totals were verified on 24 August 2026 and include fees. Airbnb repricing is real — treat anything older than a week as an estimate.
