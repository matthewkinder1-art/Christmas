The pick — two places under 200 m apart, for the fortnight

The honest catch: it is remote. Before anyone books

Three things to check

  1. Step-free from the car, not just inside. Every listing above has a ground-floor bed; none of them promises a level path from the parking space. One message to the host settles it.
  2. Two dogs in writing. The pet filter was on for every search, but get it stated on the booking — it's the rule that eliminated half of Cornwall.
  3. Prices move, and places go. Totals were re-verified on 16 September 2026 and include fees; the fortnight prices were checked the same day. Between 31 August and 14 September six places that fitted the brief were booked, including the previous partner house; since then nothing has gone, but Otters Holt, Carnawn and the Porth apartment all went up. Waiting has a price, and it is rising.
