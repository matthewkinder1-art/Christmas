The area

  • The only place anywhere on this list where both bedrooms in the main cottage are ground floor and both en suite
  • Dog walking field on site; the Camel Trail runs past the bottom of the farm
  • St Tudy, Blisland and St Breward each 1½–2 miles, each with a shop, post office and pub
  • Port Isaac 9 miles, Padstow 16, Bodmin Moor on the doorstep
  • Inland — no walkable pub, and no sea view
  • Christmas price not published; the two agencies quote different dog limits (3 vs 4)
Why it wins on paper: everywhere else we're compromising on one of Nan's three needs — ground floor, en suite, step-free. Trenarlett is the only Cornish option that gives all three, in a purpose-built accessible wet room, with room for two dogs. The cost is location: it's farmland, not coast.
