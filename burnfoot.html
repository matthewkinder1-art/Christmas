The area

  • Cragside's six-mile carriage drive is done in your own car, with lay-bys and viewpoints — no walking needed at all
  • Alnwick Garden Winter Lights runs 20 Nov–3 Jan, with free wheelchair and scooter hire
  • Dark Sky Park — you can stargaze from the doorstep
  • Barter Books in Alnwick: enormous secondhand bookshop with open fires
  • The village pub closed for good in 2023 — nearest open pub is a drive
  • Alnwick Castle is shut for the winter from late October
  • Highest snow risk of anywhere here — unlit upland lanes, low gritting priority
Be honest with ourselves about this one: it's stunning, and completely remote. No pub within walking distance, an hour's round trip to a big supermarket, and if it snows we could be stuck for a day or two. Great if "log fire and nobody around" is the actual point.
